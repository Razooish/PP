Sneg - Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

Introduction

Sneg is a Chrome extension that provides a classic Snake game directly from the browser toolbar. Your privacy is important to us, and this policy explains how the extension handles data.

Information Collection

Sneg does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information. The extension does not require an account, registration, or personal details to use.

Local Storage

Sneg may use Chrome's Storage API to save game-related preferences and records, such as your selected theme, preferred speed, and high score. This information is stored locally using Chrome's extension storage and is used only to maintain your game experience.

Data Sharing

Sneg does not sell, rent, or share user data with third parties. It does not use personal information for advertising or user tracking.

Third-Party Services

Sneg does not rely on third-party analytics or advertising services to provide its core gameplay functionality.

Data Security

Game preferences and records remain within the browser's extension storage. Users can remove this data by clearing the extension's stored data or uninstalling the extension.

Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated if the extension's functionality or data practices change. Any updates will be published on this page.
