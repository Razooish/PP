Privacy Policy

Effective Date: March 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Zoolia (the “App”) handles your information when you use the app. By using the App, you agree to this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

Zoolia is designed to minimize data collection. We do not require account registration and do not collect personal information such as your name, email address, or phone number through the App.

The App may store the following information on your device:

  • Your onboarding completion status.
  • Your favorite animals.
  • Your notification preferences (such as reminder frequency).

2. How We Use Information

Information stored by the App is used only to provide core functionality, including:

  • Remembering your app setup and preferences.
  • Showing your saved favorites.
  • Sending scheduled local notifications based on your choices.

3. Local Device Storage

Most app data is stored locally on your device. This data is not shared with us unless you choose to share it yourself (for example, by sharing content from your device).

4. Notifications

If you enable notifications, Zoolia may send local notifications to your device. You can disable notifications at any time in your device settings or in the app settings (if available).

5. Third-Party Services

Zoolia may rely on platform services (such as Apple iOS and Google Android notification systems) to deliver app features. These platforms may process technical data according to their own privacy policies.

6. Children’s Privacy

Zoolia is educational and family-friendly. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, contact us so we can address it.

7. Data Security

We use reasonable measures to protect information stored by the App. However, no method of electronic storage is completely secure.

8. Your Choices

  • You can clear app data through your device settings.
  • You can uninstall the App at any time to remove locally stored data.
  • You can control notification permissions through your device settings.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be reflected on this page with a revised effective date.
